Autopilot is installed.

ONE manual step remains: FFmpeg.
Autopilot renders video with FFmpeg, which is not bundled
(license + size). Copy your builds into:

    <install folder>\_internal\engines\ffmpeg\ffmpeg.exe
    <install folder>\_internal\engines\ffmpeg\ffprobe.exe

(Optional) Piper voices go into _internal\engines\piper\.

The Settings page in the app shows what it found. Everything else
(script editing, project management, licensing, subtitles preview,
Google Drive backup config) works without FFmpeg.

Google Drive backup (optional): see _internal\docs\DRIVE_UPLOAD.md.
